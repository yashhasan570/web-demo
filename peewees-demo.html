The house on the point

Built on the historic Pee Wee Camp site (1911), Pee Wee's opened its doors in July 1997. Owners Simon and Lily Matthews took over in 2002 with one vision: great food and service in a world-class location. Head Chef Paul Joyes and his team cook what they call Modern Territorian — the produce, climate and cultures of the Top End on one menu.
