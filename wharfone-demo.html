Casual enough for a drink, special enough for a party

Wharf One sits on the Waterfront's edge, serving wood-grilled flavours and fresh local produce from midday every day. Happy hour kicks off at 4pm, dinner service from 5pm, and there's easy bill splitting and tap-to-pay for groups. Birthdays, weddings, corporate nights — functions come with no venue hire fee.
