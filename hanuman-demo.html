The kitchen

Jimmy Shu's journey from Colombo to Australia's Top End made Hanuman the NT's most-loved Asian restaurant. Malaysian, Thai and Indian flavours served since 1992 — indoors, or under the stars on the covered terrace. Everything on this page comes straight from Hanuman's current dine-in and dessert menus.
